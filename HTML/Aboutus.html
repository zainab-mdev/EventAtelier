Who We Are

Born from a dream,
built with heart

Founded in 2022 post-pandemic, Event Atelier was born from a simple observation. As friends and family shared their frustrations about not being able to bring their dream events to life, founders Zainab and Aisha saw an opportunity to turn inspiration into reality.

With a shared passion for design and meaningful experiences, they created Event Atelier to help people transform their Pinterest boards into beautifully executed celebrations. From intimate gatherings to large-scale events, we believe that if you can dream it, we can bring it to life.

What sets us apart is our belief that event planning is an art form. No two events are ever the same, and we approach each project with creativity, care, and attention to detail — pouring our hearts into every experience we create.
